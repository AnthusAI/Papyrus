Once you have chosen the model, a Fast service tier does not make it smarter. It buys priority capacity or a nearer place in the queue. The thing it changes is how long you wait. Fast converts tokens into lower latency, and latency only costs you something when a person is sitting there waiting.

The volume is the same either way: because AI coding costs have collapsed, these things can fill a repo overnight. A human on every diff is already theater. The leftover control isn't a person staring at the patch. It's whether the secret scanner ran on this tree, before the commit.
